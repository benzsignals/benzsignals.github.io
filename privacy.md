# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy covers benzsignals.github.io, the Benz Signals Telegram channel and the Benz Signals Uploader app, a private tool that the channel owner uses to upload videos to his own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

**What we collect.** The results page uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter without cookies, to count page views. It does not collect names, email addresses or IP addresses.

**TikTok and YouTube.** The Benz Signals Uploader app only connects to the channel owner's own accounts. It uploads videos and reads view counts of our own videos. It does not access data of other TikTok or YouTube users, and nothing is sold or shared.

**Telegram.** If you join the Telegram channel, Telegram handles your data under its own privacy policy. We cannot see your phone number.

**Contact.** Questions: message us on Telegram at t.me/BenzSignalsFX.
